# public/broll: live-action B-roll slots (optional)

Drop these files here and the film uses them automatically. They are darkened by 25 %, slightly desaturated and muted, and captions and the disclaimer stay on top. Without a file, a code-built stand-in plays.

| file | section | what to shoot |
|------|---------|---------------|
| `broll1.mp4` | 回头看 … 同一件事 (≈173.7–177.8 s) | A tablet showing a candle chart, with a hand drawing a white circle and arrow around the candles. |
| `broll2.mp4` | 不是猜 … 你的每一个动作 (≈177.8–183.9 s) | Two monitors and a laptop showing live charts. |
| `broll3.mp4` | 都在跟着市场给的反馈走 (≈183.9–186.5 s) | Two hands working on a laptop keyboard. |

- Use 1920×1080 at 30 fps, at least as long as the slot. Each clip starts from its first frame.
- Any chart on screen must be **红涨绿跌** (red up, green down), as the film uses.
- Use footage you own or have licensed, and don't show third-party logos or branding.
